Bioelectronics

Wearable, Implantable, and Ingestible Systems
Yasser Khan · First edition, 2026

A textbook on how bioelectronic devices are designed. It takes a sensing capsule, a wireless ECG patch, and a brain implant apart into the same blocks (body interface, sensor, front end, converter, processor, radio, power, and package) and then teaches each block in turn, with worked examples, problems, and four labs that build one device layer by layer.

Written for Bioelectronics, a graduate course at the University of Southern California. It assumes no biology and no circuits beyond Ohm's law.
